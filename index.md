# Corethief

They come up for what's yours.

By Kevin Petersen · <kevinpetersen2110@gmail.com>

[Download on the App Store](#)

## Nothing here chips away at a life counter

Hostiles walk to your vault, **lift a core, and carry it back out**. Kill the thief on the way out and the core drifts home by itself. Let it reach a cave and that capacity is gone for good.

Your cores are not a health bar. Each one does something while it sits in the vault — adds damage, fire rate, range, scrip, or slows the ones running off with it. A thief takes a specific one, so a steal is a piece of your build walking out of the map.

### 33 towers, 66 modifiers

From a 45-scrip Bulwark to a 900-scrip Anchor Rig that mounts on bare rock and shatters the face it stands on. Five upgrade levels each.

### Towers are walls

The route bends around what you build, and a dotted line shows you where it will run before you commit. Sealing the vault off completely is the one thing you are not allowed to do.

### They learn

The game reads your damage report and counters it. Lean on one gun and Reflectors start throwing its damage back. From wave six a growing share of every wave takes the quieter road around your killbox instead of walking through it.

### 40 maps, 4 modes

A 40-level campaign across four difficulties, plus Endless, Gauntlet, Sandbox, and a Daily Seam where everyone gets the same map and the same seed.

### 255 commendations, 16 ranks

Trainee to Corethief. They unlock 25 cosmetic finishes and nothing else — no stats, no towers, no advantage.

### No ads, no purchases

The whole game. No account, no tracking, nothing to buy.

## What is down there

**34 hostiles**, ten of them bosses arriving every fifth wave from ten. Sappers that chew your towers instead of running past them. Reflectors that return what you deal. Menders that repair the crowd. A Hive that drops more of itself. From wave 42 they start arriving as elites with one of six affixes.

**Five combos** that only fire when two towers work the same target — shatter a frozen enemy, spread fire with splash, break corroded armour with pierce.

**12 hazards** on the Risk board. Make the next wave harder on purpose and get paid up front — the scrip lands the moment you send it, so you can spend it on the defense that has to survive it.

**Vault works** in three tiers. They add no damage. They make a thief stand at your vault and prise, which is time your guns get for free.

**8 veins** — one standing condition per map from level five that changes what works there.

**30 procedural music tracks**, one per map, and sixteen distinct firing sounds.

## Questions, answered

**How do I play?**

Place towers with your scrip, then press Send wave — nothing arrives until you do. Anything that reaches your vault steals a core and turns for the nearest cave. Kill it before it gets there and the core comes home.

**Why did my towers change the route?**

Towers are walls. The path recalculates around whatever you build, and the dotted line shows the new route before you confirm. You can never seal the vault off completely.

**What are the coloured squares on the map?**

Ore nodes. Build on one and that tower keeps the bonus permanently — more damage, range, fire rate, or structure. There is a full explanation under How to play.

**Is it too hard?**

Medium is the intended fight and you will finish levels bruised. Easy gives you 17 cores and a generous purse. Legendary gives you 8, and you rebuild almost nothing.

**Can I play without the internet?**

Yes. Everything runs offline — fonts, sounds and music are all built into the app. There are no network requests at all.

**How is my progress saved?**

Everything is stored locally on your device — runs in progress, records, commendations, cosmetics and settings. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

**Is there a daily challenge?**

Yes. Everyone gets the same map, the same seed and the same three challenges each day, so the run is identical for every player. Scores are kept on your device in this release.

**How do I reset my progress?**

Delete the app to remove all of its local data.

**Are there ads or in-app purchases?**

No. No ads, no in-app purchases, no subscription.

**What ages is it for?**

Everyone. No graphic content — it is machines and mining hazards.

**What devices does it run on?**

iPhone and iPad running iOS 15 or later. iPhone plays in portrait. iPad plays either way.

## Your data stays on your device

*Last updated: August 2026*

Corethief ("the App") is a locally-stored, device-only application. The App does not collect, transmit, store, sell or share any personal data with any party.

### What we store locally

The App uses your device's local storage to save your runs in progress and records; your commendations and statistics; your cosmetic choices; and your preferences such as sound, music and haptics. This data stays on your device. It is not transmitted to any server, and the developer has no access to it.

### No third-party services

The App does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, tracking or crash-reporting services. It makes no network requests of any kind — every font, sound and image it uses is built into the app itself.

### No purchases

The App contains no in-app purchases and no real-money transactions.

### Children

The App does not collect personal data from users of any age, and so complies with COPPA and similar laws by not collecting anything in the first place.

### Data deletion

All App data is stored on your device. To remove everything, delete the App — iOS will remove all of its associated data.
